# Mineshaft Tycoon Texture Pack

The official texture pack for the Mineshaft Tycoon housing on Hypixel. Works on 1.21.8 and newer.

Download the zip from the latest release and drop it in your resourcepacks folder.

## Whats included

- Every pickaxe from tier 1 to 4 and all the drills
- Crate keys tier 1 to 5
- Fortune potions
- Moon, mars and sun materials
- All 7 bludgeons
- Mystic sword, bow and pants with a look for every enchant
- All fishing rods, including every Rod of the Titans setup
